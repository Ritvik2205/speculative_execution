the fenced gadgets leak all 40 bytes, identical to unfenced. Verified the binary

is NO fence-based static discriminator for V4/SSB under this oracle.

CAVEAT / interpretation: this contradicts the ISA intent (lfence is a
serializing speculation barrier and SHOULD block the bypass). Two readings:
  (1) Trust the oracle: SSB leakage is not controlled by any statically-visible
      victim feature (fence included) -> V4 has no graph-learnable discriminator

      earlier static finding (eval/ssb_edge_investigation_2026-09-04.txt) and the

  (2) InvisiSpec's memory-dependence (StoreSet) speculation may not honour lfence

      *mitigations*, only SSB *presence*. Then lfence-fenced != a valid "safe"
      sample and a different safe-arm (or oracle) is needed for the mitigation
      axis.

Either way the fence-as-label plan is dead. The family, as designed, yields
ALL-LEAK -> no leak/safe contrast -> unusable for a discriminator. Batch stopped
(was ~3.5h/sim under contention; producing only more "leak").

Candidate salvage (not yet run): a STRUCTURAL safe arm — victim that loads the
