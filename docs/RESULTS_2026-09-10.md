# 5-seed cluster results (2026-09-10) — analysis

From `eval/cluster_out/{W3_grid,W4_ablation,real_v4,real_v4_p3}.md` (Edinburgh teaching cluster, 5 seeds, mean±95%CI).

## W3 — arch-invariance × handcrafted (the core detector question)

| config | locked | arm64 | x86 | trigger-masked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| embed + hand | 0.813 | 0.618 | 0.897 | 0.803 |
| **embed, NO hand** | **0.869** | **0.752** | **0.910** | **0.859** |
| adversarial + hand | 0.812 | 0.574 | 0.889 | 0.804 |
| adversarial, NO hand | 0.805 | 0.456 | 0.780 | 0.798 |

**Two clean, 5-seed conclusions:**
1. **Dropping the 256-dim hand-feature branch improves the model on every axis** — locked +5.6pp (0.813→0.869), x86 +1.3pp, trigger-masked +5.6pp, and most importantly **arm64 +13.4pp (0.618→0.752)**. The hand features were an x86-biased crutch that *hurt* cross-ISA generalization. This is the strongest positive: it validates the "learned / ISA-independent" thesis directly — the graph carries the signal better without hand engineering.
2. **The DANN arch adversary backfires.** Adversarial is worse than embed on arm64 in both hand (0.574 vs 0.618) and no-hand (0.456 vs 0.752) settings. Gradient-reversal arch-invariance, as implemented, hurts. **Drop it.**

**Winner config: `embed, NO handcrafted`** — best on all four axes and it is the ISA-independent direction (no hand features, no arch adversary). This should become the production/paper-headline detector.

## P3 — folding real hardware V4 into training (5-seed)

| held-out real-V4 recall (seed-disjoint, 5 gadgets) | |
|---|---|
| BEFORE (v55h, no real V4) | **0.000 ± 0.000** |
| AFTER (v55h + 11 real V4) | **1.000 ± 0.000** |

Confirmed at 5 seeds: folding 11 real hardware V4 gadgets into training takes held-out real-V4 recall from 0% to 100%. **The fix for V4 is data, not the structural edge.** (Caveat: held-out is 5 gadgets from one generator seed — coarse.)

## The complete V4 arc (publishable narrative)
1. Synthetic V4 looks solved in-distribution: 99.5% on the locked test.
2. On real silicon it collapses: **0%** recall on 16 hardware-confirmed gadgets (P2).
3. The store-forwarding edge can't rescue it — it barely fired until fixed (P3b: 3→163 edges), and even firing it gives no locked-test lift.
4. Real hardware gadgets in training recover it: **0% → 100%** held-out (P3).
Conclusion: V4 was unlearnable for lack of a real training signal, not because it is structurally invisible.
